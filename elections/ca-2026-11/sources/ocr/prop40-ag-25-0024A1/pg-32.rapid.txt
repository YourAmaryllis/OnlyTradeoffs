Sec. 8. Conflicts.
This Act provides for a tax on net worth, not income, of billionaires. In the event that this
measure and another measure that levies a tax or affects the tax rates on net worth (not
income) of billionaires, or that provides for use of funds from a tax on billionaire net
worth, shall appear on the same statewide ballot, the provisions of the other measure or
measures shall be deemed to be in conflict with this measure. In the event that this
measure receives a greater number of affirmative votes than a measure deemed to be in
conflict with it, the provisions of this measure shall prevail in their entirety, and any
conflicting provisions of the other measure or measures shall be null and void.
