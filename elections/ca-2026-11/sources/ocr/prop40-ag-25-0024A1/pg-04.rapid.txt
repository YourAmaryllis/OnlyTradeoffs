(u) The immense and growing fortunes of billionaires bring privileges inaccessible
to most. Whereas nurses, teachers, firefighters, and tech workers alike pay
taxes on nearly all of their earned income, billionaires can shield enormous
sums from taxation. Their primary assets—stocks, businesses, real estate
grow in value year after year, but because this growth is taxed only at sale and
billionaires often need not sell, they pay no tax on their rising fortunes.
