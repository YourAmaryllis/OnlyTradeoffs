(x) According to leading economists, billionaire wealth is more concentrated in

positioned to address both the well-documented crisis ofwealth inequality in
the United States and the emerging and interrelated crises the state faces with
respect to health care, education and hunger-relief programs.
(y) Billionaires have built their extraordinary fortunes with the help of California
resources and were the largest beneficiaries of the federal legislation that
contributed to the current state budget crisis. It therefore is both necessary and
equitable to ask those who have benefitted most from California's resources to
contribute proportionately to support health care, education, and nutrition in
California through a one-time 5% tax on billionaire wealth.
